Privacy and terms

PianoGuide is a free piano reference and practice site. There are no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no tracking, and nothing you do here is sent to us.

In short

  • A few settings are kept in your browser, on your device, and nowhere else.
  • The microphone is only used when you allow it, and what it hears is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub, which sees visitors' IP addresses, as any web host does. Nothing else is loaded from other companies.
  • The fingerings are common ones. If your teacher gives you different ones, follow your teacher.

What is kept on your device

To remember your choices, the site keeps a few things in your browser's storage. They stay on your device: they are never sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Light or dark mode, if you pick one with the button in the header.
  • Practice: what you last practised and your settings, and the tally of this session (right answers, skips, what to go over again). The tally is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • Metronome: the tempo, time signature, subdivision and accents, and the tempo changes you set.
  • A copy of the site itself, so it opens without the internet: its main pages and the ones you visit, or all of it (about 5 MB) once you add it to your home screen. It is the site's own files, nothing about you.

None of these are cookies, and none of them can identify you. To delete them, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

The microphone

Practice can listen to your own piano. It only turns the microphone on after you press “Allow the microphone”, and your browser may ask for your permission too.

  • The sound is checked in your browser, a few times a second, for the notes you play. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • The microphone goes off when you press Stop, switch to another tab or app, or leave the page, and after five minutes without hearing you play.
  • It never comes back on by itself: each time, the page asks again.

MIDI keyboards

If you connect a MIDI keyboard in Practice, the notes you play are read in your browser to check your answers. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., which delivers the pages through a content delivery network. Like any web host, GitHub and that network receive your IP address and the pages your browser asks for, and may keep them in logs for security. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles them.

Everything else comes from the site itself: the typefaces, the sheet music and the piano recordings. There are no analytics, ads or social media buttons.

Using the site

PianoGuide is free to use. It is made to help you practise alongside your lessons, not to replace a teacher.

  • Fingerings are the common ones, checked against published fingering charts. Many scales and arpeggios can be fingered in more than one good way: if your teacher gives you a different fingering, follow your teacher.
  • No guarantees. The site is offered as it is. Care goes into getting every note and finger right, but there may be mistakes, and the site may change or be unavailable at times.
